# Code of Conduct

Cameleer follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the
work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not acceptable in issues,
pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer privately through a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/pmsudhi/cameleer/security/advisories/new) or by
contacting the repository owner. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainers may
remove content or block participants who break this code.
